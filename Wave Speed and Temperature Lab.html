Why it works

Lightning makes two waves at the same time: light and sound. They travel at very different speeds.

Light

Light is an electromagnetic wave.

It does not need air to travel. It can go through empty space.

300,000,000 m/s

Sound

Sound is a mechanical wave.

It needs a medium, like air. Air particles bump into each other to pass it along.

about 343 m/s

Why 5 seconds?

Sound goes about 343 meters each second.

1 mile is about 1,609 meters.

1,609 ÷ 343 ≈ 5 seconds for each mile.

Who uses this?

People who work outside, coaches, lifeguards, and weather scientists use the flash-to-BOOM count to know how close a storm is.

Stay safe: When thunder roars, go indoors.

If you can hear thunder, lightning is close enough to hit you. Go inside a building or a hard-top car. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before you go back out.
