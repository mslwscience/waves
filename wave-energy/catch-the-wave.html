Catch the Wave

Can ocean waves make electricity? Let’s find out.

Waves carry energy

Wind pushes on the ocean. That push makes waves.

Waves carry energy to the beach. Can we catch that energy and make electricity?

CrestThe top of a wave.
TroughThe bottom of a wave.
Wave heightFrom the bottom to the top.
AmplitudeFrom still water up to the crest. Wave height is 2 times the amplitude.

Did you know?

The water does not ride to the beach. It goes up and down in a little circle. Only the energy moves forward. Watch the yellow dot on the next page.

Make power

A float bobs up and down on the waves. The bobbing turns a generator. The generator makes electricity for homes.

Move the sliders. Watch the power change.
