Build a path out of everyday things. Then test it and watch the sound wave travel through each material.

Sound is a longitudinal wave. Particles push on the next particles, then move back. The squeeze moves forward. Each particle only wiggles back and forth.

Sound is a mechanical wave. It needs a medium (a solid, liquid, or gas) to travel through. No particles, no sound.
